Never written stories in my life before, I set out to learn the fundamentals and hopefully craft some interesting and memorial stories, that either stand by themselves or power my coming games.
{{< /lead >}}

As in contrast to Game Development and Drawing, I do not have a course or any structure to follow - I am currently still looking for resources.

I have read that its important to get feedback on your work, so I have signed up to the forum [Mythic Scribes](https://mythicscribes.com/), which is a community for fantasy writes, here I am to submit my work to get feedback on my stories and hopefully also contribute to others for theirs.
